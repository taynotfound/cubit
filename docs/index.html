How it plays

Tap. Slide. Bump. Think.

1

Tap an arrow

It slides forward along its own winding path and off the cube, as long as nothing is in the way.

2

Mind the bumps

If something blocks it, it bounces back, and on most modes that costs a life. Turn the cube and plan.

Free the pink one

Clear its way out and tap it to win. Every level buries it deeper, from 10×10 faces up to 18×18.

One tap from the home screen

Clean mode

Nothing but the cube. It turns to glass in your colourway, with the back faces showing through and the arrows fading with depth.

  • No lives, no timer, no buttons. Just an X to leave.
  • Two-finger tap to take back a move.
  • Your clean runs are marked on the global board.
Everything else

Tucked away, there when you want it

Honest rankings

Optional. The server replays every ranked run's taps against the same cube and scores it itself. Watch any run back.

Daily cube

One 12×12 cube a day, the same for everyone, with its own board and a streak to keep alive.

Powerups

Scan, Shield, Freeze, Spare life and Undo, one each per level, all switchable. Hints are earned by playing well.

Pick up later

Leave mid-level and it's saved. Continue from the home screen, even after the app closes.

Badges and XP

Fourteen badges, player levels, an endless campaign, free roam from 2×2 to 18×18, and level replays.

Made to be played

12 colourways, a striped target that doesn't rely on colour, a left-handed layout, real haptics and a quiet soundtrack.

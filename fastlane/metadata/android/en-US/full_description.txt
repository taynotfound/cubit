<p>Cubit is a cube covered in winding arrows. Tap one and it slides off the cube, unless another arrow is in its way. Dig the pink one out before your lives run out.</p>

<p>Levels start at 10×10 per face with about a hundred arrows and grow to 18×18, and the pink arrow sits deeper every level. Arrows wrap over the edges onto the next face, so turn the cube, zoom in and plan ahead. Bigger cubes bring bombs that set off chain reactions, two-way arrows and wormholes straight through the cube.</p>

<p><b>Ways to play</b></p>
<ul>
<li>An endless campaign with five modes, from Zen (no lives, no timer) to Expert (one bump and it's over)</li>
<li>Nightmare: every arrow has to run a full lap of the cube before it leaves</li>
<li>Twist: turn a face and every arrowhead on it turns with it</li>
<li>The World Cube: one giant cube every player clears together, live</li>
<li>Endless runs, cube after cube, with a shared run of the day</li>
<li>A daily cube and a weekly challenge, the same for everyone, each with its own board, and an archive of past dailies</li>
<li>Clean mode: just a see-through glass cube in your colours</li>
<li>Puzzle packs, name cubes and free roam: any cube from 2×2 to 18×18</li>
</ul>

<p><b>And</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Par for every cube, badges, XP, a daily streak and colourways you earn</li>
<li>Race a ghost of the leader's run, or a friend's from a shared link</li>
<li>Share your result as a picture or as text; friends without Android can play shared cubes in the browser</li>
<li>Powerups, all switchable, and hints earned by playing well</li>
<li>Optional global rankings, verified by replaying every run on the server</li>
<li>Accessibility: striped target, left-handed layout, heavier arrows</li>
</ul>

<p>No ads, no accounts, and no tracking. The network is used only if you turn on rankings or the update check.</p>
